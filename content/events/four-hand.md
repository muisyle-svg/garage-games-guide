## Objective

Throw four discs into the basket, forehand.

## Setup

All discs inside the basket.

## Gameplay

Collect as many discs from the basket as desired. You must make one shot from behind each line in increasing order of distance, four shots in total. All shots must be forehand. No vertical blades or scoobers are allowed.

## Additional Guidelines and Clarifications

- If desired, shorter shots may be replaced by longer shots. For example, all four shots may be taken from the same spot behind the farthest line.
- If you run out of discs, you must collect them yourself from either the ground or the basket to continue shooting.
