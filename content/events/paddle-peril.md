## Objective

Balance the paddle on 1 hand while walking the course and back.

## Setup

Paddle on the wall.

## Gameplay

Take the paddle off the wall and balance it vertically on one or more fingers or in your palm while on the turf and behind the line. While continuously balancing the paddle, walk onto the rubber, through the squat rack, then turn around and return to the turf.

## Additional Guidelines and Clarifications

- You may not cup or squeeze the end of the paddle in your fingers/palm in any way.
- Your entire body and the paddle must fully break the plane of the squat rack before returning.
- The paddle may breifly contact obstacles, but obstacles may not be used to assist balance.
- You must demonstrate balance of the paddle while on the turf on the return before letting go of the paddle (no "falling finish").
- The paddle must be on the wall before hitting both the start and the finish buttons.
