## Objective

Sort the 4 target candies into their separate bowls.

## Setup

All candy well mixed in the large bowl and covered

## Gameplay

Dig through the bowl of candy to find 4 of each designated brand and separate them into their own bowls.

## Additional Guidelines and Clarifications

- You are looking for Skittles, Milky Way, Twix, and Gummi Bears.
- There are 6 of each in the bowl, you only have to find 4.
- You may not pick up or dump any of the bowls.
- Only target candies can exist in the target to complete the eveny
- All candy must be in a bowl to complete the event. If any candy remains on the table or ground you are not finished. 
- Do not eat any of the candy.
