## Objective

Lock the tape within the scoring zone.

## Setup

Tape extended and locked in designated zone.

## Gameplay

Keeping the measuring tape body flat on the board, you must release the lock on the tape, and as it quickly winds back into the body, use the lock to stop the tape while the flag is between the two lines of the scoring zone.

## Additional Guidelines and Clarifications

- You must lock the tape using the button and not your fingers.
- You only get one lock attempt per try, partially depressing the lock button causing the tape to slow counts as an attempt. 
- The tape body must remain flat against the board while retracting. 
- The tape must stay locked after you let go, and you may not move the tape body once locked. 
- If you are unsuccessful, you must extend the tape back beyond the starting line to try again. You may pick up the tape body while extending.
- The tape must be upright upon starting, and not be artificially slowed by any means including using the finger groove, partially pressing the lock button, or angling the tape against the ground.
