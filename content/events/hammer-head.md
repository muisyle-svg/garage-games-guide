## Objective

Hammer one nail completely flush.

## Setup

All nails in the box, hammer on the wood, safety glasses returned.

## Gameplay

Put on the safety glasses, grab a nail, and nail it completely into the board.

## Additional Guidelines and Clarifications

- You must be wearing the safety glasses to start the event.
- You may not place the nail in any previous hole.
- The nail must be hammered straight and flush with the board. If the nail bends you must either straighten it or start over with a new nail.
