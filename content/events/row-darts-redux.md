## Objective

While the seat moves forward, throw a dart and hit anywhere inside the scoring zones on the board.

## Setup

Grab your darts and sit on the rower. Do not strap in.

## Gameplay

1. Push off the ground with your legs so the seat slides backward, then lift your legs.
2. The seat must hit the end of the track. It then slides forward on rebound and/or gravity.
3. While the seat moves forward, throw a dart at the board.

## Scoring

You are not finished until you return any loose darts to the board.

## Additional rules

- Touch nothing but the seat while throwing.
- Throw one dart at a time.
