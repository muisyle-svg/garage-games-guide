## Objective

Using the keypad, enter the coordinates of the disc indicated on the TV screen.

## Setup

All discs must remain in exact starting location.

## Gameplay

When you press the start button, the TV scoreboard will display the name/description of a disc on the wall. You must input the coordinates of the disc on the keypad, Row A-D first, then column 1-15 second, then * to enter. You must successfully find two discs to complete the event.

![The Wall-D keypad: number keys 0 to 9, letter keys A to D, star and pound](images/keypad.jpg)

## Additional Guidelines and Clarifications

- Discs will always be entered Row, then Column, then *
- When you hit the start button, you will hear a chime to remind you to look at the TV.
- If you enter an incorrect coordinate, you will hear a buzz.
- After your first correct response, a second description will appear automatically.
- After two correct responses, your time is automatically recorded. You do NOT need to press the button again to finish the event.
- **Assistants may not in any way indicate the proper disc**
- To keep everyone on their toes, there are two possible disc prompts that do NOT exist on the wall. If you get one of these, enter ## then hit * to submit. This will count as a correct response.
